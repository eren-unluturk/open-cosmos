# Pitch data (Nov 7 2026): guides, lessons and honest wording
More numbers are in `docs/STATS.md`. Regenerate counts with `python scripts/guide_progress.py` and the tests.

## What the project provides
- **1,085 entries** from 3 open sources. **1,070 are visible** (475 kid-safe, 595 behind the grown-up gate); 15 adult-only are never shown.
- **Every visible entry resolves to a guide**: 65 handwritten group guides (100% of visible entries) plus 7 topic fallbacks (safety net).
- **26 adapted lessons** (ages 5-7: 12, 8-10: 13, 11-13: 1) from CS Unplugged, CC BY-SA 4.0. Everything else is link-only: **1,028 link-only entries, 57 open-content entries**.
- Guide types: 1,044 entries end in a "go to the learning site" slide, 26 end in "go to our lesson".

## Honest wording (use this on slides and in the demo)
- "CS Unplugged lessons are **adapted** under CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and a statement that changes were made."
- "For everything else we provide an **AI-written overview guide with a recommended timeline** and a link to the original site. We do not copy that content."
- "**Time estimates are estimates.** Guides were **written from catalog data only** (titles, descriptions, level, topic), not from reading the linked pages."
- "Translations of titles and descriptions are AI-generated and not yet checked by native speakers. Guides and lessons are English for now."
- "'Kid-safe' means nothing in the listing looks risky; we did not review the linked pages." For ages 11-13 on the two CC0 lists, it means beginner-level material, not content written for kids.
- "A child's progress (done items and stars) is saved only in their own browser; we have no accounts and nothing is sent to us."
